My Story

Some people pack photos when they move abroad…

Hola!
I'm
Valeria

In 2022 I swapped spreadsheets in Argentina for a tiny kitchen in Aarhus.

New country, new language, no job. So I did what Argentinians do when they miss home: I baked. First medialunas, golden and buttery. Then facturas, dulce de leche, all of it.

My friends ate everything (fast) and told me to start selling. Aarhus's Argentinian community showed up, and La Pony was born.

Why "La Pony"?

Back home my friends said I'm like a pony: stubborn, full of energy and impossible to stop. They were right.
